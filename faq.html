Frequently Asked Questions

How does the car configurator work? +

You can select a car model, customize its features, and view it in 3D before booking a test drive.

Can I save my configurations? +

Yes! You can save your configurations and revisit them later to make changes.

Do I need an account to use this service? +

No, you can explore and customize cars without an account, but you’ll need one to save configurations.

Is there an option to book a test drive? +

Yes! You can book a test drive after configuring your car by filling out a simple form.
